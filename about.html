About Eftekhar

Curious mind, lifelong learner, and someone who believes the small things matter.

Hi, I'm Eftekhar Ahamed. Welcome to my blog — a quiet space where I share thoughts about life, technology, SEO, and the small everyday moments that often go unnoticed.
