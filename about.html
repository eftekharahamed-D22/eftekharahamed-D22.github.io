About Eftekhar

Indian by heart, polished in Dubai.

Hi, I'm Eftekhar Ahamed. I live and work in Dubai, where I lead Facilities Management initiatives for luxury developments and branded residences.

My background is engineering. My discipline, over thirteen years, has been the unglamorous craft of making complex systems run beautifully — quietly, reliably, day after day.

I believe great work is invisible. Behind every well-run building, every well-led team, every well-served community, there are unseen hours of attention and care. That is the work I'm drawn to.

This blog is where I think out loud — short reflections on nature, the journey, and the spiritual moments between.

Find me on LinkedIn, or simply say hello.
