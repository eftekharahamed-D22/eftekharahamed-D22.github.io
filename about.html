About Eftekhar

Engineer · MBA · Facilities Management Leader — building communities where service meets precision.

Hi, I'm Eftekhar Ahamed — based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, and currently serving as Assistant Manager — Facilities Management at Strata Global Holdings, a leading Owner's Association Management Company in Dubai.

For more than 13 years, I've worked at the intersection of engineering and operations — helping luxury developments, branded residences, and high-profile communities run with the precision and care they deserve.

My Professional Story

My career began as a Technician with Signals & Systems in India, working on GPS sync devices and distance protection relays across 132kV, 220kV, and 400kV substations in Northern India — including the National Thermal Power Plants and state electricity boards.

From there, I moved to Dubai, where I grew steadily — Facilities Coordinator and Facilities Engineer at QBG Facilities Management, Facilities Lead Executive at Khidmah in Abu Dhabi, Chief Engineer — Facilities at Dutco Group, and now Assistant Manager — FM at Strata Global.

Along the way, I've had the privilege of managing portfolios for some of the region's most respected names — Wasl, Aldar, MIRAL, Jumeirah Golf Estates, ENOC Headquarters, Saudi Consulate, Clover Bay Tower, NTPC, and even contributing to operations within the highly regulated environment of the UAE Presidential Royal Palace.

Today at Strata Global, I lead strategic and operational initiatives for luxury developments and branded residences managed for Owner's Associations — including Accor, MAG, Select Group, RKM, Durar, Dar Global, and Volante.

What Drives Me

I believe that great facilities management is invisible — it's what allows people to live, work, and thrive without ever thinking about the systems holding everything together. My commitment is to understand before we react, to lead with integrity, and to leave every community better than I found it.

I'm particularly passionate about sustainable practices and energy management — small, consistent choices that compound into meaningful impact for the planet and the communities we serve.
