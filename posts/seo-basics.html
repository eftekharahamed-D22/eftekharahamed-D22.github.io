SEO Basics Everyone Should Know

Search Engine Optimization sounds complicated, but the fundamentals are simple. If you have a website, understanding these basics can make a big difference in how many people find you.

What is SEO?

SEO is the practice of making your website more visible in search engine results. When someone searches for something on Google, SEO determines whether your site shows up on page 1 or page 100.

The Three Pillars of SEO

1. On-Page SEO

This is about optimizing the content on your pages:

  • Title tags — Every page needs a unique, descriptive title (under 60 characters)
  • Meta descriptions — A short summary that appears in search results (under 160 characters)
  • Headings — Use H1 for the main title, H2 and H3 for sections
  • Quality content — Write for humans first, search engines second
  • Internal links — Link between your own pages to help users and search engines navigate

2. Technical SEO

This is about making sure search engines can find and understand your site:

  • Fast loading speed — Slow sites rank lower and lose visitors
  • Mobile friendly — Most searches happen on phones
  • SSL certificate — HTTPS is a ranking factor
  • Clean URLs — Use readable URLs like /seo-basics not /page?id=123
  • Sitemap — Helps search engines discover all your pages

3. Off-Page SEO

This is about building your site's reputation:

  • Backlinks — Other websites linking to you is like a vote of confidence
  • Social signals — Shares and mentions on social media
  • Brand mentions — People talking about you online

Quick Wins You Can Do Today

  1. Add your site to Google Search Console (it's free)
  2. Make sure every page has a unique title and meta description
  3. Check your site speed with Google PageSpeed Insights
  4. Make sure your site works well on mobile
  5. Start creating useful content that answers questions people are asking
SEO is not about tricking Google. It's about making your site genuinely useful and easy to find.

Start with these basics and you'll be ahead of most websites. SEO is a long game — be patient and consistent.
